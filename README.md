# Dear Mother
## Credits
Asher, Kelcy, Patrick, Matt, Arshvir
## Story
After advancements in technology created AI that strayed from its original purpose of assisting humanity, the world’s resources and life were slowly consumed by four autonomous robot factories called Mothers. The ground-level mother is the Mother of Preservation. She was corrupted to freeze herself and not intervene with the world, as preserving everything is an impossible task. Beneath the Mother of Preservation was the Mother of Dispersion, tasked with Dispersing Robots generated by the lower factories across the world. She started stealing other factories’ robots and making them her own, using them as a personal army. Below her was the Mother of Recycling, who was corrupted to recycle life that was still alive. The bottom Mother was the mother of Propagation, tasked with creating robots. She was corrupted to run the world out of resources by creating veritable armies of robots. Each mother lays claim to one of the four floors of a massive industrial complex that burrows deep into the earth. Depravity and decay reign until the Mother of Preservation’s core directive is re-initialized, turning her against her kin. 

This Mother creates you—our player—to venture deep into the complex and destroy the other three mothers. You play as iterations of a robot, finding and equipping useful items from enemies you’ve vanquished on the floors below. You have a limited number of item slots, so you will have the option to scrap these items. This scrap can be sent up to your Mother after successfully killing a Mother or traded with NPC traders in-run for items or to regain health. This scrap, if sent up, can be used in between runs to permanently upgrade your Mother’s factory which improves the features of the next iteration of yourself. You will traverse rooms of equal size until you find the mother’s room, which is a larger room where a boss fight takes place. After defeating the mother, an elevator door will open to allow you to descend to the next floor.

As you delve deeper into the bowels of the factory, foes become increasingly out of touch with the natural world, aggressive and corrupted by the twisted core directives of the mothers that created them. This creates a ramping difficulty. The three hostile mothers will be different (assets, attacks, etc). You will find that the complex is so large that every time you venture out, you find a different path to descend toward its core. Occasionally, you may stumble upon dead iterations of yourself from which you can recover scrap, robots who have abandoned their mothers (NPC traders) and other interesting rooms.
